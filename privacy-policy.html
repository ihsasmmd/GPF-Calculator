Privacy Policy

GPF Calculator — Last updated: August 2026

GPF Calculator is a personal General Provident Fund ledger and calculator. This policy explains, plainly, what happens to your data.

What data the app collects

None. GPF Calculator does not have a server, does not use analytics or tracking, and does not require an account or sign-in of any kind.

Where your data is stored

Everything you enter — your name, personal number, GPF reference number, BPS, and every year of ledger entries — is stored only on your own device, in the app's local storage. It is never transmitted anywhere, to us or to anyone else.

Backup and PDF export

If you use the "Backup Data" or "Export PDF" features, the app creates a file on your device and hands it to your phone's normal share sheet or downloads folder. Where that file goes after that is entirely up to you — the app itself does not upload it anywhere.

PIN lock

If you turn on the optional PIN lock, only a one-way cryptographic hash of your PIN is stored on your device — never the PIN itself — and it is used solely to unlock the app locally.

Third parties

The app does not integrate any third-party analytics, advertising, or tracking services.

Deleting your data

The app includes a "Delete All Data" option (on the Cover tab) that permanently erases everything stored on your device. Uninstalling the app has the same effect.

Changes to this policy

If this policy changes, the "Last updated" date above will change accordingly.
